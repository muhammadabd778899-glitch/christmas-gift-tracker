## Full description

**Plan Christmas without a spreadsheet, an account, or a subscription.**

Christmas Gift Tracker is a browser-based gift planner for one person or one household. It keeps the people,
the gifts, the budget, and the ideas together, and it stores everything on your own device.

**What it helps you do**

- Keep one active Christmas season with a holiday date and an overall budget.
- Track recipients with a relationship, an optional spending cap, and notes.
- Plan gifts with a category, store, link, planned budget, actual cost, and notes.
- Follow each gift through bought, wrapped, and sent, with dated status toggles.
- See season budget, planned spending, actual spending, remaining budget, and unallocated money at a glance.
- Save gift ideas in an Idea Bank, assign them to a recipient, and convert them to gifts when you decide.
- Build a shopping list from gifts that are still unbought.
- Search, filter, and sort gifts, recipients, and ideas.
- Archive past seasons as read-only records and start a new season when you are ready.
- Download a JSON backup, restore it later, or export gifts and recipients as CSV.
- Choose light, dark, or system theme, and set your own currency and number format.
- Install the app to your home screen or desktop for a focused, full-window view.

**Offline by design**

This is the offline edition. There is no sign-in, no account, no cloud sync, and no analytics. After the
first online visit, the app is cached and works without a connection. The footer shows whether you are
online and confirms that data is saved on this device.

**Your data stays with you**

Recipients, gifts, ideas, budgets, and notes are stored in your browser's local database. Backups and CSV
exports are created on your device. No copy is sent to the seller or to a third party. Because there is no
cloud copy, regular JSON backups are important. Clearing browser data can remove the planner.

**What you receive**

This is a digital download. It contains:

- START-HERE.pdf - a short setup guide.
- User-Guide.pdf - every screen, totals, backups, and troubleshooting.
- License-and-Support.pdf - terms, privacy summary, and support details.
- Sample-Data/Sample-Backup.json - an optional example season you can restore and explore.
- Offline-Edition/INSTALL.txt - install notes you can keep next to the app.

The app address is supplied with your purchase. You need an internet connection once to open and cache the
app. This is an installable web app, not a native desktop or mobile app, and it is not opened from the
downloaded folder.

**Requirements**

- A current Chrome or Edge browser is recommended. Microsoft Edge 156 on Windows is the configuration
  verified for this release.
- Android Chrome can install the app to the home screen. iPhone and iPad steps are included, but Safari
  behavior was not verified for this release.
- Firefox on a computer can use the app in a tab after the first online visit; it does not offer installation.

**Good to know**

- No account and no cloud sync in this edition.
- No automatic off-device backup. Download a JSON backup regularly.
- One active season at a time; older seasons are archived.
- This is a planning tool, not financial, legal, or tax advice.
- The sample season is clearly flagged and can be removed without affecting real records.

**Support**

Questions about installation, offline setup, or a reproducible defect? Contact the seller through Etsy
Messages using the account that bought the product. Include your app version (1.0.0), browser and device,
the steps you took, and what happened. The seller does not request remote access to your device or your
planner data.
